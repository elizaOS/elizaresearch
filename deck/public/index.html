One caregiver's week: six days of coordination for 3 dialysis appointments.

Three treatment days become a six-day-a-week job for her. When we asked what she'd hand off first, this was her answer.

03 · Why today's assistants can't help

AI assistants are built for one person. Senior life involves many.

04 · What we're building — product concept, being validated in research

The same week, with Eliza on the team.

1 · Schedule
Eliza holds the standing schedule, a year of appointments, and knows what tomorrow requires.
2 · Wait for the call
Eliza does the waiting. Transport confirms to Eliza, not to a 2:30 PM alarm in her head.
3 · Chase
If the confirmation hasn't come by 2:30 PM, Eliza makes the call and sits on hold. She gets a text when it's resolved.
4 · Brief him
Eliza calls him to remind him about tomorrow's schedule, and if he wants to check again, he asks Eliza instead of calling his sister.
5 · Cover the day of
When the driver is late, he tells Eliza. Eliza informs his sister and contacts transportation on his behalf, escalating only if a decision is needed or the issue stays unresolved.
6 · Recover
Problems surface at 2:30 PM instead of at pickup time, so the 120-mile day doesn't happen.

Which of these steps families would trust an agent to handle is exactly what our research is validating.

05 · Why this matters now

Families already spend $70B+ a year on senior independence.

$15B
Home modification services
Installing ramps, walk-in showers, stair lifts (Harvard JCHS, U.S.)
$35B
Assistive devices
Walkers, wheelchairs, hearing aids, home hospital beds (IMARC)
$17B
Watch & respond
Medical alert buttons, fall detection, in-home sensors (Towards Healthcare; Dataintelo)
$5B
Connect
Simplified phones and tablets built for seniors, like the Jitterbug (Data Insights Market)

$70B+ buys safety and simplicity. Independence — the coordination behind it — still runs on family labor. And the products aren't landing: 60% of adults 50+ say technology isn't designed with their age in mind, while 71% want age-tailored support (AARP 2026).
